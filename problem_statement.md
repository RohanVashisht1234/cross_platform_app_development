40. MealCraft (Recipe Sharing & Meal Planner App)
Problem Statement:
MealCraft wants an app where a user browses recipes, adds favorites to a weekly meal plan, and
automatically generates a combined ingredient list for the week, all stored locally on the device.
(With Proper Justification)
Objectives
● UI/Widgets: Build Recipe Browse, Recipe Detail, and Weekly Meal Plan screens using
GridView, Card and ListView widgets.
● Styling/Theming: Apply an appetizing Material 3 theme with clear recipe imagery and
ingredient typography.
● Dart Logic: Model Recipe as a Dart class with an ingredients List, and merge multiple
recipes' ingredients using a Map for the weekly list.
● Figma: Design a clean, guided flow covering every screen listed above with progress cues
for the user.
Show recipe photo, name, prep time, and difficulty level on every Recipe Browse card.
Outcomes
● Adding a recipe to a day in the Weekly Meal Plan uses Recipe objects stored in a
structured list.
● The app auto-generates a combined ingredient list, merging duplicate ingredients via a
Map.
● Weekly Meal Plan screen shows a day-by-day grid of planned recipes.
● All recipes and the current meal plan persist between sessions using local storage.
Deliverables
– Figma Design – Recipe browse through meal-plan flow.
– UI/Widgets – Recipe grid, detail screen, and weekly plan grid.
– Styling/Theming – Appetizing Material 3 recipe theme.
– Dart Logic – Recipe class and Map-based ingredient merging.
– Local Storage – Persisting recipes and the weekly meal plan.
– Responsive Prototype – Working browse-to-meal-plan cooking journey.
